Two findings, both fixed:

  - sections/articles ran to 186 characters, past where a search snippet is
    cut. It was being truncated mid-clause on the one page whose whole job is
    to tell a reader what four articles are about.
  - pages/home sat at exactly 160, which is not wrong today and fails on the
    next word anybody adds.

Nothing was a placeholder and no ogDescription was missing, so the audit half
of this checkpoint found less than the enforcement half. That is the finding
worth recording: eight heads were written by hand, eight were fine, and there
was no reason to believe the ninth would be.

ogImage is absent on all eight, deliberately. Per-section social images are
S7.1's checkpoint, and the schema keeps the field optional until then.


2. WHERE THE BAR NOW LIVES
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
src/content/schema.ts — the shared `meta`, so one bar covers every collection
and the editor refuses a bad edit at the same moment the build does:

  description     60..160   the snippet a search result shows
  ogDescription   45..120   the line a social card has room for
  neither may equal the title
  canonical       /^\/([A-Za-z0-9-]+\/)*$/  site-relative, both slashes

src/lib/collections.ts — the half a schema cannot see, because only the build
knows the routes: an entry's `meta.canonical` must be the URL it is served at.
The route base is not a constant; it is the section page's own canonical, so
`/runs/the-fleet-round/` is checked against the `/runs/` that runs.yaml
publishes and a section that moves takes its entries with it.

test/meta.test.mjs — six tests: the bar bites at both ends, neither line may be
the title, a canonical is a directory path, and annotation coverage.

That last pair is the reason the test exists, and it is a correction to what
this repo believed: `checkAnnotations` does NOT silently ignore an unannotated
page — it warns twice and then passes. Right call for a kit shared across a
fleet, where a site may scope a page it has not finished annotating. Wrong one
here, where a page that stopped being editable is a regression. The test is
that warning promoted to a failure, not a gap being filled.

All three breaks reverted; `git status` clean of them before the commit.


5. WHAT THIS CHECKPOINT DID NOT DO
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
- No ogImage anywhere; S7.1.
- The 60/160/45/120 bounds are the published truncation points of somebody
  else's renderer, not something measured from this corpus. They are a house

  recomputed from anything. Said plainly here because the site's first rule
  applies to its own gates too.
